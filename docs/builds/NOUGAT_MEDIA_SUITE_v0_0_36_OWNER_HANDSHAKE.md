1. Home `Continue Watching` artwork fills its landscape viewport proportionally and the Local/Recommended poster cards fill their portrait artwork viewport without the prior black-gap/offset defect.
2. Library has the exact sheet-style `Search` box on its own row below the green buttons, with placeholder `Search`; typing filters the visible local titles.
3. Recognized movie collections appear once at the Movies root and open to their member films rather than duplicating all member movies beside the collection card.
4. Player seek uses the approved-sheet appearance at the shorter width with elapsed time on the left and total time on the right.
5. Moving/dragging the mouse no longer alternates between clipping the top of VOLUME and clipping seek/percentage text.
6. The accepted VOLUME component itself remains unchanged in size/art, with no rectangular pale patch outside its rounded housing.
7. The global header uses the VOLUME-housing tan; the left brand and right Server/version cluster are vertically centered; the Server lamp uses the sheet-family circular indicator.
8. Live TV scan still finds/stores the already validated channels. `Watch Live` tuning/playback is deliberately the next-build agenda, not a v0.0.36 claim.
