# Nougat Media Suite v0.0.21 Brand and Palette Contract

## Suite identity
- Product name: **Nougat Media Suite**.
- App icon: approved rounded-square chocolate/nougat **N + play triangle** artwork.
- Common chrome: deep cocoa/chocolate, cream text, caramel/gold accents.
- Top-level labels: `Video Player | Library | Discover | Search | Stream | Debug`.

## Area palettes
- Video Player: deep chocolate, cocoa, cream, caramel/gold accents.
- Library: evergreen, forest, sage, parchment.
- Discover: deep plum, violet, lavender, soft white.
- Search: dark cocoa, chocolate, nougat cream, caramel/gold.
- P2P sub-surface inside Search: midnight/navy, steel/cobalt, ice blue.
- Debug: graphite/brown-black, amber/gold, parchment.

## Stream service-reactive palettes
- YouTube: `#FF0000` with charcoal/black/white.
- Rumble: `#85C742` with dark green/near-black/white.
- RuTube: `#ED143B`, `#100943`, `#123AED`, `#9A13ED`, `#12CCED`.
- VK: `#0077FF` with dark navy/white/cyan highlight.
- OK: `#EE8208` with chocolate/dark neutral/cream.

The Stream URL field, actions, focus border, status area, log panel, and selected-service treatment follow the active service palette.

## Scope boundary
v0.0.21 is the official identity/palette release plus the owner-approved Search/P2P navigation consolidation and Library view-control repair. No new Stream service is added. Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, Kick, TikTok, Bilibili, Niconico, and Jelly/Unity candy-button sprites move to v0.0.22.
